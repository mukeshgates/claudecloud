### 1a. The four flagged claims in detail

**Milei, "non-human corporations".** Real and on the record in his own op-ed, about June 2026. Critics in Argentina call it "programmed impunity: human gains, social harm and responsibility shifted onto machines" (quoted in coverage). The legal point: these entities could have their own legal personhood and limited liability, so AI agents could sign contracts and move money. Legal experts say Argentina would be the first country to allow this. Yuval Noah Harari publicly objected, and Milei promised a reply. Only one small YouTube news video covers it ("Argentina Plans Legal Framework For AI-Run Companies", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sOm-_BD2riM). **This is open ground.**

**Olah and the pope.** Per the NYT (29 Sep 2026), via Futurism and aggregators:
- Pope Leo XIV invited Olah to help present his first encyclical, *Magnifica Humanitas*, at the Vatican's Synod Hall on **25 May 2026**. The pope signed it on 15 May, the anniversary of *Rerum Novarum*.
- Olah saw an advance copy and proposed that Anthropic withdraw over its line that AI lacks experience and conscience. He appeared on the dais anyway.
- His team then privately lobbied the **pope's advisers** to take machine consciousness seriously. The final text **did not change**.
- This was part of a wider Anthropic outreach running since autumn 2025: private meetings with theologians, many under NDAs. People named include Rabbi Mois Navon, Charles Camosy, Meghan Sullivan and Wakanyi Hoffman. Camosy has since concluded that models are not conscious.
- On stage, Olah said "We find internal states that functionally mirror joy, satisfaction, fear, grief and unease." To the NYT he later said: "we don't know if A.I. models are conscious. I don't know. I'm genuinely uncertain."
- Wording risk: outlets differ on whether Olah "proposed" or "threatened" to withdraw. Say "lobbied the pope's advisers", not "lobbied the pope".

**Poll figures (91/74/73).** All three numbers appear in one Quinnipiac release (Sept 30 2026):
- **91%** say it is very or somewhat important for the US to set guardrails for AI systems.
- **74%** have "not much" (29%) or "none at all" (45%) trust in the leaders of AI companies.
- **73%** are very (38%) or somewhat (35%) concerned that AI could threaten human survival.
- Same poll: 86% want independent safety standards even if they slow development. 77% say companies should slow or stop powerful AI until safety can be evaluated. 52% worry more about humans using AI to cause harm, 33% about autonomous AI.
- Sources: https://www.ibtimes.com/almost-three-four-americans-fear-ai-could-threaten-human-survival-new-poll-shows-3808125, https://courthousenews.com/most-americans-dont-trust-ai-companies-to-regulate-themselves/, https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/10/01/poll-finds-broad-support-for-ai-safety-standards-despite-competitive-concerns/. The primary should be on poll.qu.edu.
- Caveat: the 73% question measures *concern* about a possible risk, not a prediction. A different 91% exists: Verasight, April 2026, 91% back "some level" of AI regulation (https://betanews.com/2026/04/19/us-voters-ai-risks-rewards/). Confirm which one the op-ed cites.

**Thiel on the pope.** At the Aspen Ideas Festival (CNN, 2 Jul 2026), Thiel argued that Pope Leo's encyclical, which calls for AI to be "disarmed" and strictly regulated, would slow only the US side of the AI race because Beijing would ignore it. On that logic the pope was effectively, even inadvertently, "working for the Chinese Communists".
- The audience laughed.
- He also called the AI concerns a distraction from the Church's abuse crisis (https://moneywise.com/news/top-stories/peter-thiel-pope-leo-ai-vatican-regulation).
- Fordham's David Gibson said it shows how Thiel reacts to anyone who sets limits on him (opinion).
- Sanders replied on X: "Who do you trust? Pope Leo or Mr. Thiel?" (https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/07/60323228/bernie-sanders-asks-who-do-you-trust-the-pope-or-peter-thiel).
- Christopher Hale has said Thiel previously speculated that Leo could be an Antichrist figure. That is UNVERIFIED as a direct Thiel quote.
- **On camera, use "working for the Chinese Communists" and show the "inadvertent / race with China" logic.** Never say "called him a Chinese agent" without that framing.

## 5. Risks: what must be attributed as opinion, and how

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| "Terrible plan", "oligarchs", "terrifying plans" | Sanders' opinion. Say "Sanders argues..." |
| "Transhumanism" as the ideology behind them | An interpretive label. Most of these people don't use it. Present it as Sanders' and critics' reading. |

| Musk "empathy is a weakness" | Give the immigration context and his "I believe in empathy" line. |
| Amodei | Present as a warning he is raising, not as Anthropic's goal. |
| Andreessen "murder" | Quote exactly. It is about slowing AI, not about people. |
| Persona / Discord | Founders Fund backing is fact. Do not imply Palantir software or data sharing. |
